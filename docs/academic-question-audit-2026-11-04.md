# Academic question report review — 4 November 2026

Reviewed 112 reports in Chinese, English and German. P7-100 was excluded from this review at the owner's request; its previously released content is unchanged.

The review checked original figures against their interpretations, source methods and results against reported parameters and conclusions, and translated terminology and quantitative conditions. Corrections include sample units, material and salt identities, test conditions, time points, group comparisons, figure panels and legends, and German technical terminology.

Where an original article's narrative conflicts with its tables or figures, the report identifies the conflicting evidence rather than silently replacing the published value. Some sources were accessible only through original abstracts or partial full text; those checks do not establish full-text verification. Detailed source-access and correction records are retained in the project's local audit archive.

All 113 released reports remain available in the three languages, with shared original-paper titles, source links and figure assets. Library identity, citations and local assets were validated before release.
